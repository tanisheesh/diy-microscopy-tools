# Blood Smear Slide Spreader

A 3D-printable jig for making blood smears. The base slide (75 × 25 × 1 mm) sits in a recessed bed. A carriage runs along rails and holds a glass spreader slide at **45°**. Push the carriage forward and it makes the smear.

The carriage is **captive** in the base:
- **It can't lift off:** its dovetail-T tongues are trapped in undercut grooves in the rails.
- **It can't come out the front:** the grooves are closed at the front end (end stop).
- **It can't come out the back:** a removable pin goes in there.

---

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `spreader_model.py` | Parametric CadQuery model. All dimensions and tolerances are in the `PARAMETERS` section |
| `base.stl` | Base plate, in print orientation |
| `carriage.stl` | Carriage, in print orientation (lying on its side) |
| `params.json` | Derived dimensions for the viewer (generated by the script) |
| `index.html` | Standalone three.js viewer |

## Regenerating the model

```powershell
cd C:\Users\Tanish-Poddar\Downloads\smear-spreader\smear-spreader
py -3.14 spreader_model.py
```

Before exporting the STLs, the script checks:
- Minimum wall thickness ≥ 1.6 mm, and clearances within printable range (0.15–0.6 mm)
- No interference between carriage, base, pins and both glass slides at 5 travel points
- Captive fit: lifting collides with base; pin and front stop block both ends
- Each part is a single valid solid

After exporting, it checks:
- **Watertight:** every edge shared by exactly two triangles (0 non-manifold edges)
- **Normals face outward**
- **Volume matches CAD model** within 1%
- **Sits flat on bed** at z = 0

## Print settings

| Setting | Base | Carriage |
|---|---|---|
| Material | PLA or PETG | PLA or PETG |
| Layer height | 0.2 mm | 0.2 mm (0.16 gives more accurate tongue) |
| Walls / perimeters | 3 (1.2 mm) | 3 |
| Infill | 20–25% gyroid | 25–30% gyroid |
| Supports | **None needed (0%)** | **None needed (0%)** |
| Orientation | Flat on bed | On its side |

---

## Assembly & Usage

1. Place the base slide in the bed.
2. Slide carriage tongues into grooves from the rear end.
3. Cut two pieces of 1.75 mm filament (~11 mm each) and push straight down into pin holes.
4. Place drop of blood on base slide, insert 45° spreader slide, and push carriage forward smoothly.
