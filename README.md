# Blood Smear Slide Spreader

A 3D-printable jig for making blood smears. The base slide (75 × 25 × 1 mm) sits in a recessed bed. A carriage runs along rails and holds a glass spreader slide at **45°**. Push the carriage forward and it makes the smear.

The carriage is **captive** in the base:
- **It can't lift off:** its dovetail-T tongues are trapped in undercut grooves in the rails.
- **It can't come out the front:** the grooves are closed at the front end (end stop).
- **It can't come out the back:** a removable pin goes in there.

**Live viewer:** https://tanisheesh.github.io/smear-spreader/

## Regenerating the model

```powershell
cd C:\Users\Tanish-Poddar\Downloads\smear-spreader
py -3.14 spreader_model.py
```

Before exporting the STLs, the script checks:
- Minimum wall thickness ≥ 1.6 mm
- No interference between the carriage, base, pins and both glass slides, at both ends of travel
- Captive fit: lifting the carriage collides with the base, and the pin and front stop block both ends
- Each part is a single valid solid

If any check fails, no STL is exported.

CadQuery 2.8 is installed in the global Python 3.14 (64-bit), so run the script with `py -3.14`. The default `python` is 3.15 32-bit, and there is no CadQuery wheel for it.

Key parameters:
- `SPREADER_ANGLE`: spreader angle (currently 45)
- `SLIDE_L/W/T` and `SPREADER_L/W/T`: base slide and spreader slide sizes
- `CLR_SLIDE`: clearance for the slide bed and spreader slot (0.3)
- `CLR_RAIL`: clearance between tongue and groove (0.25)
- `GROOVE_*`: groove shape
- `PIN_*`: stop pin size and position
- `BASE_L/W`: base size

## Key dimensions

- **Base:** 100 × 44 × 9 mm.
  - It was 40 mm wide originally. It had to grow by 4 mm to fit the captive groove's undercut with 1.6 mm walls on both sides.
  - Plate top at z = 4 mm.
- **Rails:** 8.6 mm wide, 5 mm above the plate.
- **Groove (dovetail-T):**
  - 2.6 mm neck at the top, with a 1.6 mm lip.
  - Below the lip it widens at 45° to 5.4 mm. Total depth 3.0 mm.
  - Closed for 4 mm at the front end (end stop).
- **Carriage tongue:** 0.25 mm smaller than the groove on every face. It sits 0.8 mm under the lip on each side. If you try to lift the carriage, it stops after 0.35 mm.
- **Stop pins:**
  - Two pieces of 1.75 mm filament, each about 11 mm long.
  - They go into Ø1.8 × 3 mm holes in the groove floor, 4 mm from the rear end.
  - Printed holes usually come out about 0.1 mm small, so the pin is a light press fit and won't fall out even if the base is turned upside down.
  - Each pin sticks out about 5 mm above the rail, so you can grab it and pull it out.
- **Slide bed:**
  - 75.6 × 25.6 mm, 0.8 mm deep.
  - The slide sits 0.2 mm above the plate, so the spreader rides on glass, not plastic.
  - Ø12 mm finger notches at both ends for lifting the slide out.
- **Spreader slot:** 45°, 25.6 × 1.6 mm. The spreader slides down under its own weight and rests on the base slide.
- **Smear travel:** about 48 mm (contact X = 36 to 84.5 mm).

## Print settings

| Setting | Base | Carriage |
|---|---|---|
| Material | PLA or PETG | PLA or PETG |
| Layer height | 0.2 mm | 0.2 mm (0.16 gives a more accurate tongue) |
| Walls / perimeters | 3 (1.2 mm) | 3 |
| Top / bottom layers | 5 / 4 | 5 / 4 |
| Infill | 20–25% gyroid | 25–30% gyroid (the knob takes the push force) |
| Supports | **None needed** | **None needed** |
| Orientation | Flat, bottom face on the bed (the STL is already like this) | On its side, one cheek on the bed (the STL is already like this) |
| Brim | Not needed | 3–5 mm brim recommended, since the part stands 44 mm tall |

Why these orientations:
- **Base:** the groove undercut is a 45° overhang, so it prints without supports. The pin holes are vertical.
- **Carriage:** printing on its side puts the slot, knob and tongue profile all in the bed plane. The tongue's 45° faces print without supports, and the slot roof is only a 1.6 mm bridge.

Material notes:
- **PLA:** the most accurate and easiest to print.
- **PETG:** handles disinfectant wipes better and is less brittle. Turn on elephant-foot compensation (0.1–0.2 mm), or the tongue may be tight in the groove.

Fit tuning:
- If the carriage runs tight, set `CLR_RAIL` to 0.3.
- If the spreader sticks in the slot, set `CLR_SLIDE` to 0.35–0.4.
- Then run the script again.

## Assembly

1. Place the base slide in the bed.
2. Slide the carriage's tongues into the grooves from the **rear end** (where the pin holes are) and push it forward.
3. Cut two pieces of 1.75 mm filament, about 11 mm each. Cut them straight with a cutter so the ends are flat.
4. Push each piece straight down into a pin hole until it sits on the bottom of the hole. A finger is enough. If it's tight, grip it with pliers and twist gently while pushing.
   - The carriage now can't lift off or come out of either end.
5. **Removing the pins:**
   1. Pull the spreader glass up out of the slot.
   2. Move the carriage forward a little so there's room near the pins.
   3. Grip the exposed ~5 mm of the pin with your fingers, tweezers or small pliers.
   4. Twist gently and pull straight up.
   - The carriage then slides out the back. You can reuse the pins or cut new ones; filament is cheap.
6. **If the holes print too tight** and the pin won't go in:
   - Twist a 1.8 mm drill bit through the hole by hand.
   - Or sand the end of the filament slightly thinner.
   - If the holes are too loose, after inserting the pin, warm its top end with a lighter and bend it into an L shape.

## How to use

1. Move the carriage back to the pin.
2. Pull the spreader slide up out of the slot.
3. Put a drop of blood on the base slide, just under the front edge of the carriage.
4. Put the spreader back in the slot. It slides down by itself and rests on the slide just in front of the drop, and the blood spreads along its edge.
5. Push the carriage forward by the knob in one smooth motion.

> Lab safety: wear gloves when handling blood samples. After use, disinfect the parts and glass according to your lab protocol.
